### A4. Comparing two ingredients to see shared pairings
- **Rating**: [2 ]
- **Notes**: I can only seem them in the ingredients tab, but i want to be able to see them in the model visualization too. but none of the ingreients are labeled, which is fine when it's in the default mode, but when i select an ingredient i'd like to be able to see the labels.  also it's too easy to click out of the comparison because in order to get a detailed look i have to spin the model or zoom in which causes it to either accidentally click on another ingredient or exit out of the ingreidients i'm observing.

### A5. Overall visual quality (bloom, glow, particle animations, dark theme)
- **Rating**: [4 ]
- **Notes**: it's a little bright in the center of it where there is the most traffic, but over all it's a very pleasing aesthetically. i love the particle features and the way the flavors are color coded and clump together in their flavor clusters because it makes it so visual.
