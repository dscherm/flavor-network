What to look for

  • Does the dish or drink stay recognisable, or does it turn into noise? Busy backgrounds are the usual cause.
  • Which look sits best beside the app's hand-drawn icons?
  • The gold figure under each look is its file size at the size a shared recipe would store. Under about 100 KB means it can live in the database beside the recipe, with no separate file storage.
  • Try a dim photo, a top-down plate, a drink against a window, and one with people in the background.
