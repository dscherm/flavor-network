Six ways to draw a flavor profile

A recipe now has six tastes and eleven aromas, seventeen notes in all. Each design below is drawn from the numbers the app works out today for the same five recipes, once as it would sit in the app at phone width and once on the link-preview share card, the tightest card there is.

Every chart is scaled to the recipe's own strongest note. Notes scoring under 0.05 count as not present.

What I would choose

D on everything a visitor sees, A where you are working, F for before and after.

  • Share cards and the shared page get the tasting note with strips. It needs the least room and nobody has to learn a chart.
  • The Recipe Notebook, the invent panels and the Flavor profile button keep ranked bars, because order and strength matter most while you are adjusting a recipe.
  • Suggestion cards get the fixed grid, since two grids side by side show exactly which notes an ingredient moves.
  • The flavor map wheel either goes, or becomes design C.

If nothing is chosen, design A stays as it is now on every surface. Any single design can also be used everywhere. Mixing three costs more to build and keep consistent than using one.
