Neural Flavor: what goes under the hat

The name, the chef's hat and "Nf" stay exactly as they are. The atom under the hat is redrawn as something from a kitchen (A to C) or, as the owner asked to see, a lab beaker (D). Each is drawn in the hat's chalk line, with the logo's own orange and sage. Below each: the mark at landing size, then at 64, 32 and 16 px as the full mark scaled down, and as the small-size cut that would become the favicon.
