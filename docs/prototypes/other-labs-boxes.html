Beef Bolognese

ItalianPasta Serves 4Time: 2½ hr8 ingredients
Flavor profile Share

Slow-braised meat ragu over pasta.

About this dish

Bolognese sauce, known in Italian as ragù alla bolognese or ragù bolognese, is the main variety of ragù in Italian cuisine, typical of the city of Bologna.

Ingredients

  • 500 g mincedbeef
  • 400 g cannedtomato
  • 1onion
  • 1carrot
  • 1 stalkcelery
  • 2 clovesgarlic
  • 150 mlred wine
  • 400 g tagliatellepasta

Method, 5 steps
  1. 1.Finely chop onion, carrot, celery and garlic; soften in oil over low heat 10 min.
  2. 2.Raise the heat, add the beef and brown it well, breaking it up.
  3. 3.Pour in the wine and let it bubble away.
  4. 4.Add the tomatoes, season, and simmer very gently, partly covered, 2 hours.
  5. 5.Cook the pasta, toss with the sauce, and serve with parmesan if you like.
