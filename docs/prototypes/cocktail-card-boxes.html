What changed

  • The double frame around the whole card is gone: the opened card is the board itself.
  • Back, Riff on this, Flavor profile and Share lose their boxes and the family-red fill; they are text controls in one row.
  • One filled button: Add to shopping list. Copy shopping list and I have it are text controls.
  • Cocktails like this and the cousins are plain lists of names (dotted underline, as ingredient names already are), not pills.
  • Sections sit 40 px apart; rows inside a section 8 px apart. Method numbers move from family red to faint chalk.
