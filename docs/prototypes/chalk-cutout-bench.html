Chalk Cut-out Bench

Two ways to lose the background of a chalk picture. Focus fades everything outside a centred oval. The automatic cut-out asks a small model, running in this browser, which part of the photo is the subject, and draws chalk only there. Nothing is uploaded: the photo never leaves this browser.

You can also drag a picture onto the page, or paste one.

0 keeps the whole photo. Higher keeps a tighter centred oval.

What to try

  • A plated dish on a table, shot from above and from the side.
  • A drink in a clear glass. Clear glass is the hard case: the model may keep only the liquid, or lose the stem.
  • A busy background: a bar shelf, a kitchen counter, people behind the plate.
  • Two things in one photo. The model picks what it thinks is the main subject and may not agree with you.
  • The time under the switch is how long the cut-out took on this device. A phone will be slower than a laptop.
