Common requests

  • Delete my account / data — Email us with the subject line "Delete account" from the address you signed in with. We confirm within 30 days; backups are wiped after a further 7-day Firestore retention window.
  • Export my data — Email us asking for an export. We send back a JSON dump of everything we hold about you within 30 days.
  • Bug or crash — Email us with a screenshot, what you clicked or tapped, and your browser and device. Or open an issue on GitHub.
  • Feature request / feedback — Tell us what you'd want to see. Especially: missing ingredients, wrong pairings, classical dishes/cocktails/sauces we should add.

What's in the app?

  • Pairing Model — 3,913 ingredients × 48,588 pairings, scored from 2.2M real recipes.
  • Cocktail Model — 172 cocktails, seven super-families.
  • Sauce Model — 77 sauces, ten mother families.
  • Recipe Lab — build a bowl, get next-ingredient suggestions ranked by how reliably they cook with what you've already added.
