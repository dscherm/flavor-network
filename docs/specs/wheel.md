## WHEEL-1 — The ingredient card draws the combined flavor wheel

PairingModeCard.jsx is the chalk single-ingredient card, shown in the Pairing Lab and (since BUILDER-4) as the IngredientCard overlay everywhere an ingredient name is tapped. Its chart is MiniRadar, a polygon radar over guidedRadarAxes with a filterType and a chosenAxis. Replace it with the combined wheel: FlavorWheel fed with that ONE ingredient's seventeen notes from the current flavor tables (the per-ingredient values behind profileForRecipe; src/data/flavorMarks.js already reads them per ingredient for the flavor-map marks; do not use the old model values and do not add a second calculation), so an ingredient's wheel and a one-ingredient recipe's wheel agree. Same wedge positions, colours, labels and scaling rule as everywhere else; a size that fits the card at 375 px without clipping any label (the present radar clips 'pungent'; that must be gone). Where the Pairing Lab passes a chosen filter or axis (filterType, chosenAxis), keep the information without bringing back the polygon: mark the chosen note's wedge (a stated, tested treatment such as an outline and its label always shown) and say in the report what each old filter type maps to and what could not be mapped. When the card shows a PARTNER against the ingredient you came from, state what the wheel shows (the partner's own notes, as the default) and keep the shared-compounds section as it is. An ingredient with no flavor data shows the same plain 'not enough flavor data' line the other surfaces use, not an empty ring. A text equivalent (the ranked list of notes) for screen readers, as FlavorWheel already provides. Remove MiniRadar and any helper only it used, after a grep. Do not change what else the card shows.
