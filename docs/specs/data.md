# data as-built specs

## DATA-1 — Repeatable data audit with today's baseline

scripts/auditData.mjs reads only repo files and reports: duplicate ingredient candidates (misspelt singulars such as 'tomatoe', plurals, leading preparation words such as 'freshly', 'lean', 'boneless', 'cooked', 'instant'), non-food and fragment candidates, branded and packaged candidates, category inconsistencies by rule (cheese, juice, flour, wine, beer, the 'umami' category), taste-label oddities by category (starch, plain fat, water, neutral spirits), cocktail checks (cream or egg drinks per family, citrus in the stirred families, lines without amounts, duplicate names, unnamed sub-groups, a short list of classics that must exist), the share of sauces under 'Independent', dish-type singletons and the two dish-type vocabularies. Writes docs/data-quality.md (method, limits, baseline numbers) and candidate lists as review files under data/review/. Changes no data.

## DATA-2 — The curation layer: aliases, hidden, packaged, category and taste overrides applied on load

src/data/curation/ with empty-but-valid tables (aliases.json, hidden.json, packaged.json, categoryOverrides.json, tasteOverrides.json; each entry one line with a reason) and a pure applyCuration(ingredients, pairings, tables): aliases merge an entry into its canonical one (pairings combined, the stronger score kept, counts summed, self-pairs dropped), hidden entries removed with their pairings, packaged entries flagged, overrides applied. Wired where useProData builds the graph and into every build script that reads ingredients (share index, placeholder list). resolveIngredient(name) goes through the alias table and is used by the typed-ingredient matchers, barKey, kitchenKey and the model vocabulary lookups, so an old or variant name still lands on the canonical entry. A saved recipe that names a merged or hidden ingredient still opens and shows its original text. Seed the tables with ten obvious entries to prove the path end to end.

## DATA-3 — Ingredient merges, removals and packaged flags

Fill aliases.json, hidden.json and packaged.json from DATA-1's review lists after reading every line: duplicates (at least the 151 found on 2026-11-15) merged into their canonical entry; non-food and fragments hidden; branded and packaged products flagged (Decision 3: kept and searchable, never suggested). Where a 'duplicate' is a real distinction a cook would care about (new potato vs potato, extra-virgin olive oil vs olive oil, cherry tomato vs tomato), it is NOT merged and the reason is recorded in a keep list so the audit stops flagging it. Suggestions, stand-ins, checklist picks and the bar and kitchen typeaheads exclude packaged and hidden entries.

## DATA-4 — One consistent category scheme

Write the category scheme down in docs/data-quality.md (each category with a one-line definition and three examples), then apply it through categoryOverrides.json: all cheeses under dairy; all juices under a new 'juice' category; all flours under grain; still, sparkling, fortified and cooking wines under a new 'wine' category; beer and cider under 'beer'; the 'umami' category renamed 'savory seasoning'; vermouths and aromatised wines decided once and stated. Before changing anything, list every place in the code that reads a category name (inventSauce.js slot rules, dishSkeletons.js, recipeRoles, recipeMains.js, myBar.js isBooze and family rules, suggestion filters, colour and legend maps, tests) and update them in the same change so no slot or filter silently empties.

## DATA-5 — Taste labels: add neutral and rich, fix starches, fats and spirits

The taste vocabulary gains 'neutral' and 'rich'. tasteOverrides.json corrects: starches and plain grains (flour, rice, bread, pasta, cornstarch, oats, tortilla) to neutral; plain fats and oils to rich (butter 'rich sweet' at most); water, ice and soda water to neutral; vodka neutral; other spirits, wines and beers reviewed one by one; egg, cream and cheeses reviewed; the audit's other oddities resolved or kept with a reason. Add the missing bar entries (rye whiskey, cola, common bitters and mixers) as curated additions with category and taste. Everything that reads the taste field (node colours, taste compatibility, filters, legends) handles the two new words. Must land before FLAVOR-3, which then treats neutral as contributing to no taste axis and rich as a mouthfeel note outside the six tastes.

## DATA-6 — Cocktails: a family for flips and cream drinks, misfiled drinks, names, duplicates, missing classics

Per Decision 4 (the owner may override before this starts): add a seventh family, 'Flips & Cream Drinks', with a root drink and a one-line blurb, and move the cream and egg drinks into it after reading each one (about 59 candidates). Move the clear mistakes (Mojito, Bramble) and review the drinks with citrus in 'Boozy Sippers' and 'Aromatic Stirred'. Name the 15 sub-groups. Remove the two duplicates. Add Espresso Martini, Long Island Iced Tea and Sex on the Beach with published specs. Add amounts where a standard published spec exists (IBA for the official ones); otherwise leave the existing '~ suggested' amounts. All of it in the codex's source and proDataset/scripts/11-parse-cocktail-codex.js, regenerating cocktail_codex_v2.json; the generated file is not edited by hand. Update everything that assumes six: the family list and order, the Invent style picker, blurbs and checklist skeleton for the new family, the parked 3D layout, share slugs (existing links must keep working), tests and copy that says 'six'.

## DATA-7 — Sauces: split the catch-all; one dish-type vocabulary

Review the 27 sauces under mother sauce 'Independent' and regroup them into named groups wherever at least three share one (candidates: vinaigrettes, cold emulsions, herb sauces, fermented and chili condiments, dairy sauces); the rest stay 'Independent'. Update the Sauce Lab's family list, colours, Invent a sauce techniques where a new group maps to one, and the share index. Separately, define one dish-type list used by both the Cookbook (dishTypeOf, 22 types today, six with one dish) and Invent a recipe (dishSkeletons.js, 8 types): every Cookbook dish maps to a type that Invent also knows, or to an explicit 'other' with a reason.

A test fails when the app's tables and the pipeline inputs drift. Do NOT re-run the full pipeline. Re-run the audit and record before and after in docs/data-quality.md; regenerate share-index.json and share-cards.json; update README.md and CLOSEOUT.md (unreleased entry) with the new counts (ingredients, pairings, cocktail families) wherever the old ones are quoted, and BACKLOG.md with what was deliberately left (the full rebuild, low-count ingredients).
