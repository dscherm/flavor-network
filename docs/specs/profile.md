## PROFILE-1 — A flavor profile for every existing recipe, opened from the recipe card next to Riff

Owner, 2026-11-16: 'add a task to create a flavor profile for every existing recipe and make it available in the recipe card next to the riff button'. Every built-in cocktail, sauce and Cookbook dish, and every item the user saved or invented, gets a flavor profile computed by the one shared function (profileForRecipe: taste labels and strengths, the aroma table, amounts, garnish) and shown through the shared display rule (Taste and Aroma groups, strongest first, '+N fainter notes', 'show all'). A 'Flavor profile' button sits next to 'Riff on it' on the recipe card the labs use (LabNodeCard in the Cocktail Lab and Sauce Lab) and in the equivalent action row of the Cookbook's recipe detail; it opens the profile in place (an expanding section or a small sheet in the chalk language), with the line 'Built from taste labels and aroma notes for each ingredient, weighted by amount. A guide, not a taste test.', the ingredients that drive the two strongest bars, and any ingredients not counted. Built-in profiles may come from the precomputed share data where that is already loaded, otherwise they are computed on open; either way the numbers must equal the shared page's for the same recipe. A recipe with too little data says so instead of drawing a chart. This uses the interim chart style; CHART-1's redesign will restyle it with the other charts. Run after DATA-6 (same card component).
