# standin as-built specs

## STANDIN-1 — Stand-ins: four kinds of answer, amounts, the recipe the row came from, and a coverage measure

Groundwork before any new entries. (1) MEASURE: scripts/measureStandIns.mjs (npm run measure:standins) counts, over the built-in cocktails, sauces and dishes, every ingredient line by the app's own canonical name (the resolver the shopping list uses, not the printed label), and reports per kind: distinct ingredients, lines, share of lines with an answer, the most used ingredients with none, and how many more are needed for 80%. --json. Record the baseline in docs/stand-ins.md. (2) KINDS: an entry in src/data/kitchenSwaps.js may be one of four kinds: 'swap' (another ingredient: shallot for onion; today's entries), 'form' (the same thing in another form: dried thyme for fresh, bottled lemon juice for fresh, garlic powder for a clove), 'make' (make it from things: simple syrup from sugar and water; buttermilk from milk and lemon; today's `plus` entries are this), and 'none' (nothing replaces it, with a one-line reason: eggs in a custard). Each may carry `ratio` (a number the code already supports) or `amountNote` for a conversion that is not a single factor ('1 tsp dried for 1 tbsp fresh', '1/8 tsp powder per clove'), and a short `note`. Existing entries keep working unchanged. (3) WHO MAY USE WHICH: the shopping sheet (shoppingAlternatives.js, StoreAlternatives.jsx) shows all four, labelled in plain words ('Use instead', 'Another form', 'Make it', and for none: 'Nothing really replaces X' with the reason, instead of today's 'No stand-in I'd trust'). Recipe-side users (findSwap in whatDoINeed.js and myKitchen.js, the invent panels' replacements, inventSauce.js) keep their present behaviour and must not silently start counting a recipe as makeable through a 'form' or 'make' entry unless you show that is correct for that caller; say per caller what you decided and test it. The Sauce Lab strictness test (a swap never contradicts saucePantry.sameRoleAndKind) must still hold for 'swap' entries. (4) THE ROW'S RECIPE: a shopping row records the recipes it came from (item.from). Use that so swaps flagged supportingOnly (butter and olive oil for the pan) are offered when the ingredient is NOT a main of any recipe the row came from, and withheld when it is a main of one or when the row has no recipe; show the reason in the sheet ('fine for frying; not if butter is the point of the dish'). State how main is decided (reuse the existing mainOf logic) and what data the sheet loads to know it. (5) 'make' on the list: picking it replaces the row with its parts by the merge rule (as the buttermilk case already does), with Undo. Add NO new stand-in entries in this task beyond the minimum needed to test each kind.

## STANDIN-2 — Stand-ins for food: answers for the ingredients dishes and sauces use most

Write entries in order of use until at least 80% of dish lines and 80% of sauce lines have an answer on npm run measure:standins (an honest 'none' with its reason counts as an answer and is reported separately from real stand-ins). Work down the measure's most-used list; expect roughly 100 entries. Each entry is ONE reviewable line with role and kind as today, written from common cooking knowledge and marked as the assistant's judgement, never inferred from category alone ('no basil? use mint' is the mistake to avoid). Expected content includes: garlic (jarred or powder as forms, with amounts; shallot is not a stand-in for garlic), ginger (ground, with amount; galangal only where honest), fresh and dried herbs both ways with amounts, tomato forms (canned, paste plus water, fresh), tomato paste, stocks and bouillon, soy sauce (tamari; coconut aminos), fish sauce, vinegars, mustards, chilies and chili powders and flakes, cayenne and paprika, cumin and coriander (ground and whole), cinnamon, nutmeg and mace, garam masala and curry powder as 'make' from spices, cornstarch and flour as thickeners with amounts, baking powder as 'make' from baking soda and cream of tartar, eggs as 'none' with the reason, flour as 'none' unless a specific honest swap exists, rice and noodles and pasta shapes, proteins of one kind (chicken thigh and breast, pork cuts, white fish), milk and cream and coconut milk, cheeses of one kind, nuts and seeds, sesame oil (none: it is a flavouring, not a fat) and other oils, wine in cooking, sugar kinds. A SECOND CHECK, not a source: scripts/checkStandIns.mjs lists, for every 'swap' entry, how many pairing partners the two sides share (the everyday-pairs index measureKitchenMakeable.mjs already uses) and flags the weakest tenth for a second look; each flagged line is either changed or kept with a reason in docs/stand-ins.md. Entries that only hold for a supporting role are flagged supportingOnly; entries that do not hold in a sauce are flagged sauce:false and the strictness test must pass. docs/stand-ins.md gains the full table grouped by kind of ingredient, so the owner can read and correct every line.

## STANDIN-3 — Stand-ins for the bar, docs and the final pass

Bar answers for the shopping sheet and the cocktail builder's 'Don't have it?'. Today only base spirits of one family have alternatives (shoppingAlternatives.js via cocktailRiff.swapCandidates with a family filter). Add reviewed GROUPS, each a named list whose members stand in for each other, with a note where the swap changes the drink: orange liqueurs (triple sec, Cointreau, curacao, Grand Marnier, orange liqueur); dry and blanc vermouths; sweet vermouths; bitter red aperitifs (Campari, Aperol and the like, with 'sweeter, less bitter' notes); aromatic bitters and orange bitters; fizzy waters, which are the same thing (soda water, club soda, seltzer, carbonated water) and tonic, which is not; ginger beer and ginger ale with a note; syrups as 'make' (simple syrup, rich or demerara syrup, honey syrup, grenadine from pomegranate juice and sugar) and as swaps with notes; citrus juice forms (fresh and bottled) and lemon for lime with a note; sparkling wines; cream liqueurs; coffee liqueurs; herbal liqueurs only where honest (Chartreuse: none, with the reason); maraschino liqueur (none; it is not cherry brandy); absinthe and pastis; egg white (aquafaba, with amount); cream kinds; gin and vodka (other bottles of the same spirit are the same ingredient to the app; say what the sheet should tell the cook for 'the store has no gin': an honest none, or vodka with a strong note, and justify). Decide where these live so the cocktail builder and the shopping sheet read ONE table (extend kitchenSwaps with bar entries, or a sibling table read through the same alternativesFor), and keep the existing rule that a base spirit never crosses families. Target: at least 70% of cocktail lines answered on measure:standins, real stand-ins and 'none' counted separately; if 70% is not reachable honestly, stop at what is and say why. Then: docs/stand-ins.md complete (bar table, final numbers), docs/shopping-list.md, README line, BACKLOG (SHOP-OPEN-4 closed; what was left out), CLOSEOUT.md UNRELEASED with deploy targets. Final pass at 375px on the local site: a shopping row for garlic, eggs, butter from a recipe where it is supporting, simple syrup, triple sec and gin; the cocktail builder's replacement for an orange liqueur. Save and restore any localStorage touched; never clear a list you did not create.
