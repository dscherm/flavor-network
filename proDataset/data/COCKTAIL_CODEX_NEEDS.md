# Cocktail Codex — Cocktails Needing Recipes / Confirmation

**Purpose:** This is the punch list for the new Cocktail Lab. Edit freely
and I'll re-read after your edits.

## How to use this doc

1. **Filling in recipes** — for any cocktail under "I Need Recipes," type
   ingredients + measurements under it. Free-form is fine
   (`2 oz rye`, `0.75 oz lemon juice`, etc.). I'll parse and normalize.
2. **Confirming the cluster tree** — under each family I've grouped
   cocktails into the codex's subclusters
   (`core & seasoning`, `balance`, `variations`, `extended family`).
   For families where I couldn't read your photo clearly, I've made my
   best guess. Mark with `✅` to confirm, `❌ → <new subcluster>` to
   move, or `~~strikethrough~~` to delete.
3. **Adding cocktails I missed** — append under the right subcluster.
4. **Sharper photos** — for any family I marked "blurry," a clearer
   photo will let me autoclassify the rest.
