# chemDataset — Current Status (as of 2026-04-22)

Injected into every Claude Code session via `CLAUDE.md` @-include.

## Active model
- **M3 multi-task MPNN** (GINEConv, 3 layers, hidden=128), retrained R10-63 with 5-fold CV, 15 epochs, batch=64, seed=42, CPU.
- Artifact: `flavor-gnn/artifacts/cv_results.json`
- Output consumed by app: `public/proDataset/gnn_entropy.json` (per-ingredient probs + entropy), `public/proDataset/gnn_compounds.json`, `public/proDataset/gnn_positions.json`.

## Coverage
- 2,790 ingredients have GNN predictions.
- 1,123 of 3,913 (29%) ingredients have no odor prediction — skewed toward high-pairing hubs (nut, cheddar, bacon, egg, mayonnaise, vegetable oil, tomato paste, etc.).
- ~56% of pairings have both endpoints scored. ~34% of strong pairings (strength ≥ 0.5) have a dominant shared odor class above 0.2.

## Known issues
- **Prediction collapse.** Pairwise odor-vector cosine similarity p50 = 1.000 — the head regresses toward class priors on all ingredients outside the well-represented classes. This is why salt+butter shows "woody" as dominant.
- **Hub gap.** The training subset excludes top-pairing hub ingredients, reducing coverage where users most frequently look.
- **Duplicate cluster labels.** `public/proDataset/cluster_labels.json` has two clusters both labeled "Chili" (see R11-DISCOVER-DEPTH task 1).

## UI surfacing policy (R11)
Until R12 retrain lands with F1 ≥ 0.5 on 4+ odor heads:
- Do **not** surface GNN odor head predictions as per-pairing badges.
- **Do** surface `bridge_compounds.json` FlavorDB molecule-level odor tags (human-auditable, 924 edges). Coverage lower but quality honest.
- Bitter + fruity heads are strong enough to surface elsewhere (e.g., ingredient-level filters) if needed, but not as a pairing badge.
