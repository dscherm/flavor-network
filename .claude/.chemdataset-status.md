## Active model
- **M3 multi-task MPNN** (GINEConv, 3 layers, hidden=128), retrained R10-63 with 5-fold CV, 15 epochs, batch=64, seed=42, CPU.
- Artifact: `flavor-gnn/artifacts/cv_results.json`
- Output consumed by app: `public/proDataset/gnn_entropy.json` (per-ingredient probs + entropy), `public/proDataset/gnn_compounds.json`, `public/proDataset/gnn_positions.json`.

## Coverage
- 2,790 ingredients have GNN predictions.
- 1,123 of 3,913 (29%) ingredients have no odor prediction — skewed toward high-pairing hubs (nut, cheddar, bacon, egg, mayonnaise, vegetable oil, tomato paste, etc.).
- ~56% of pairings have both endpoints scored. ~34% of strong pairings (strength ≥ 0.5) have a dominant shared odor class above 0.2.

## Known issues & root-cause update
- **Threshold collapse (R12 diagnosis):** the apparent "prediction collapse" in
  R11 was actually a thresholding artifact. The model emits confident positives
  (p > 0.85–0.95) only on true positives for rare tasks, and the blanket 0.5
  threshold discarded that signal. Per-task calibration recovers F1 >= 0.46
  on 9 of 11 tasks.
- **Data-limited tasks:** salty (16 total positives, 0.4% of dataset) and
  odor_spicy (196 positives, 4.7%) are still weak even with calibration.
  These need either data augmentation (more labeled compounds) or task
  removal from the UI.
- **Hub gap.** The training subset excludes top-pairing hub ingredients,
  reducing coverage where users most frequently look. Still stands.
- **Duplicate cluster labels.** Fixed in R11 — cluster_labels.json now has
  "Chili (cumin)" and "Chili (ginger)".

## UI surfacing policy (updated R12)
Calibrated GNN predictions now have F1 >= 0.46 on 9 of 11 heads. Ready to
ship as an **ingredient-level** signal (not per-pair), complementary to the
existing `bridge_compounds.json` per-pair odor badges.

Shippable surfaces:
- IngredientPanel: add a "Predicted profile" section reading gnn_entropy.json
  probabilities, thresholded per-task via odor_thresholds.json. Show tasks
  where probability > calibrated threshold.
- Ingredient taste radar: layer calibrated probabilities on top of curated
  taste data as a separate color, for comparison.

Do **not** surface salty or odor_spicy even with calibration (F1 <= 0.40
and <= 16 / 196 training positives respectively — likely to mislead).

Follow-ups still open:
- Hub ingredients (1,123 of 3,913) have no GNN prediction at all.
- Focal loss retrain may yield further lift on odor_spicy + odor_floral.
