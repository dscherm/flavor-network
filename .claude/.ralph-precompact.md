## Constraints — measured, do not relearn

- **`authDomain` MUST stay `neuralflavor.firebaseapp.com`.** Changing it to
  `neuralflavor.web.app` breaks Google sign-in for everyone with
  `Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch` — authDomain also sets the OAuth
  redirect_uri, and only the firebaseapp.com form is registered on the OAuth
  client. The ITP theory that motivated the change was **disproven**: Safari
  on iPhone works fine cross-origin. Do not revisit.
- **apple.news never exposes the publisher URL.** Verified on two real links
  straddling the paywall boundary (America's Test Kitchen = News+,
  Epicurious = free): identical interstitials, no canonical, no og:url, zero
  non-Apple URLs. `findPublisherUrl()` is a tested hedge that has NEVER
  fired. Don't debug it — Apple exposes nothing.
- **The matcher is not comprehensively fixed.** Reading the corpus still
  shows misses. Fixtures live in
  `src/data/__tests__/parseRecipeIngredient.test.js`.
- **Proxy share was 50% on real traffic.** `node scripts/weblink-proxy-report.mjs`
  reports it. If it stays >50%, the second-provider question is live —
  allorigins/codetabs were measured and are NOT viable alternatives.
